The future has always been uncertain, and the introduction of AI systems, including machine learning and autonomous agents, has thoroughly muddied the waters. With the massive volume of information surrounding the subject, we felt that we could add to the discussion by sharing our views on what we consider the most pressing areas of the world AI impacts.

[The first area](https://jonesy37.github.io/phil4830-site/environmental.html) encompasses the impacts that AI infrastructure has on the environment. Conversations about electricity and water usage by large data centers come up often in public discourse, along with questions about emissions and e-waste. The sheer size and scale of AI infrastructure is unprecedented and the reporting on these areas makes it difficult to understand exactly where issues lie. What we do know, is that data centers require massive quantities of water and electricity, sometimes in areas where there are already limited resources.

[The second area](https://jonesy37.github.io/phil4830-site/social.html) involves the myriad social impacts that these systems can have, often changing the way we interact as a society. Generative AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Midjourney have made it easier to spread misinformation, plagiarize, and infringe on copyright. One effect that is very curious is that of substitution. How often is a chatbot being asked questions that used to be asked to parents, grandparents, siblings, friends, etc. These questions are part of the ways we build and maintain relationships. The role of AI in our society needs to be considered carefully and critically.

[The third area](https://jonesy37.github.io/phil4830-site/safety.html) of focus are the safety concerns regarding machine learning and autonomous agents, especially around cybersecurity and biotechnology. Rising capabilities within these models creates inherent risk, and increasing the deployment of autonomous machine agents without a high standard of safety presents a massive danger to humanity. The danger of rogue AI systems has increased dramatically, as we have seen just in the past year with OpenAI’s hugging face incident and Alibaba’s ROME AI incident. Simultaneously, the AI companies themselves have utilized emotion-driven language to fuel hype around their products, drawing attention away from genuine safety concerns.

[The fourth area](https://jonesy37.github.io/phil4830-site/geopolitical.html) includes the major international considerations and implications for AI, and how AI itself is affected by both national and global regulation. Two global superpowers, China and the USA, are engaged in an arms race towards more capable AI agents on all fronts, arguably disregarding safety in favor of a rapid acceleration of development. It is essential that we have an internationally coordinated response to this issue, superior to that of nuclear and climate change, which predicate this type of global unity. This response is already garnering attention and support from researchers within the AI community as well as general policy makers, but requires a larger shift towards robust regulatory systems.

[The last area](https://jonesy37.github.io/phil4830-site/long-term.html) we highlight regards the long-term impacts AI will potentially have on our world.With this highly uncertain future, we cannot guarantee the outcomes, and we don’t need to; we must prepare responsibly as future risks increasingly become present harms. We need to respond with coordinated societal action to mitigate these risks, and there are several key principles we can follow to ensure our response to an uncertain future is the best possible path we can tread. It is important to separate grandiose visions of utopias and dystopias from the realities that are increasingly being realized.

AI, in the broadest sense of the term, has already been the source of massive change in our world, and is poised to accelerate that trend in the coming years. We believe that every person with a stake in humanity's future should be informed about the potential futures AI will influence, especially when it comes to these five areas of focus. Right now there is not nearly enough action responding to the harms that these systems perpetuate throughout the world, something that must change as AI systems are deployed in greater capacities in our society. We must act, as governments, as individuals, and as a people.
