## Data Centers
AI models, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini are primarily trained and deployed in large data centers. These data centers are outfitted with state of the art processing chips and extensive cooling systems in order to meet the models compute requirements and to remove heat generated by the hardware. Data centers have been built all over the globe, though they are highly concentrated in developed nations such as the U.S., Canada, China, and the U.K., with the worlds largest AI data center (40,000 acres, or, 2.5x the size of Manhattan) approved to be constructed in Box Elder County, Utah.[^1] While the current method of increasing a models capabilities is to increase the amount of compute it can utilize, this comes at a cost to the environment, particularly the area that surrounds these data centers. 

We can break the enviromental impacts down into 5 areas: Energy, Water, Emissions, Hardware, and Reporting. With energy, water and emissions being the main concern.
