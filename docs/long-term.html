Long Term

We can’t know exactly how or when the capabilities of AI will progress, but we can predict what will happen if they do.

Low risk of superintelligence, main harms come from a general purpose machine agent, but superintelligence is always a possibility general AI systems act exponentially faster than humans do, thus requiring significantly higher thresholds for alignment and safety

The serious nature of this problem has been weaponized on many occasions by the frontier AI companies themselves, who use “doomer” language to assign credibility to their claims the capability of their models. In 2019, OpenAI claimed that their model GPT-2 was “too dangerous to release,” going on to release it later that year. Anthropic has followed the same pattern in the way they’ve handled their new “Mythos” tier models, which they originally did not release for the same reason of it being “too dangerous.” Despite their alleged concern, they created a paid program in which the most powerful tech companies could access the model, and then went on to release it to the general public with some limitations and a new name: “Fable.”
